Delete Your PAO Account

How to delete your account and data

You can permanently delete your PAO (پاؤ) account and its associated data at any time, directly inside the app. No email or waiting period is required.

Steps to delete your account in the app

  1. Open the PAO app and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Read the confirmation message and tap Delete to confirm.
This action is permanent and cannot be undone. Once you confirm, you are signed out and your account cannot be recovered.

What is deleted

The following data is permanently deleted from our database as soon as you confirm:

DataDetails
AccountYour login (email and password)
ProfileName, email, phone number, bio and profile photo reference
ListingsAll items you posted
Requests and messagesRequests you sent or received and the messages you sent
WishlistItems you saved
FeedbackRatings and reviews you gave or received

What may be kept

Image files you uploaded (profile photo and item photos) are stored separately from your account records. After deletion they are no longer linked to any account or listing, but the files themselves may remain in storage for a short period. To have them removed, email us at shahabmustafa18@gmail.com and we will delete them. Apart from this, we do not retain any of your personal data after your account is deleted, unless we are required to by law.

Can't access the app?

If you can no longer open the app or sign in, email us from the address you registered with at shahabmustafa18@gmail.com with the subject "PAO account deletion request", and we will delete your account and data.
