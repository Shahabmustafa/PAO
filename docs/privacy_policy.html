Last updated: September 18, 2026

PAO ("we", "our", "the app") is a give-away marketplace where people post items they no longer need for free, browse items others are giving away, and arrange the handoff through in-app chat. This policy explains what the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

1Information We Collect

Information you provide directly:

  • Account details: your name, email address and password (the password is handled by our authentication provider and is never visible to us in readable form).
  • Profile details: optional phone number, bio and profile photo.
  • Listings: the photos, title, description, category, condition and province you add when you post an item.
  • Requests and messages: requests you send for items and the chat messages you exchange with other users.
  • Wishlist and feedback: items you save, and the star ratings and reviews you give or receive after an exchange.
  • Push notification token: a device token from Firebase Cloud Messaging, generated automatically while you're signed in, so we can notify you about new messages and requests. It is not linked to your device's advertising identifier and is removed when you sign out.

Information stored on your device:

  • App preferences such as your theme (light/dark) and language.
What we do not collect: we do not access your GPS/precise location, your contacts, or your call/SMS data. We do not use advertising or analytics SDKs, and we do not show ads.

2Camera and Photos

The app asks for access to your camera or photo library only when you choose to take or select a photo (for your profile picture or for an item you are posting). Photos are not accessed in the background.

3How We Use Your Information

  • Create and manage your account and sign you in.
  • Show your profile, display listings, and let you filter listings by province.
  • Let you send and receive requests and chat with other users to arrange a handoff.
  • Maintain your wishlist, ratings and reviews.
  • Remember your app settings.
  • Send push notifications to your own signed-in device about new messages and requests.

4What Other Users Can See

  • Public to other users: your name, profile photo, the items you post (including photos and province), your donation count, and ratings/reviews.
  • Shared with the other party only: messages in a request conversation.
  • Private to you: your email address, phone number and bio.

Please do not share sensitive personal information in listings or chat. We do not sell your personal information.

5Service Providers and Storage

Your account and app data are stored and processed by Supabase, our backend provider, which we use for authentication, database, file storage and real-time updates. Data is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Supabase acts on our behalf and we do not share your data with any other third party.

While we take reasonable steps to protect your data, no method of storage or transmission is 100% secure.

6Your Choices and Rights

  • Review and update your profile at any time from Settings > Edit Profile.
  • Delete your account and data from Settings > Delete Account. See the account deletion page for the steps and what is removed.
  • You can also contact us at any time to ask questions about, or request deletion of, your data.

7Data Retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, your profile, listings, requests, messages, wishlist and feedback are permanently removed from our database.

8Children's Privacy

PAO is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest revision date is shown at the top of this page.
